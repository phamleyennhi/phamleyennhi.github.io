Nhi Pham

I'm a PhD student at the Max Planck Institute for Informatics in Saarbrücken, Germany, where I am advised by Prof. Bernt Schiele. I also closely collaborate with Dr. Jonas Fischer, Prof. Adam Kortylewski, and Prof. Jan Eric Lenssen. My current primary research area is video generation and embodied world models.

Before this, I received my B.S in Computer Science and Mathematics at New York University, where I worked on computational linguistics with Prof. Adam Meyers, and on operator theory with Prof. Ilya Spitkovsky. I also interned at Goldman Sachs, Amazon AWS AI, and Meta (Facebook).

Featured Research

My current research interest lies in geometric representation learning, with an emphasis on interpretable and robust vision models grounded in 3D structure. I am also interested in extending these ideas to video diffusion models, where learning holistic, geometry-aware tokens can support structured reconstruction and generation over time. In my previous life, I enjoyed playing around with special matrices and their eigenvalues.
